Proximity

A field test for how you do closeness.

Axis I — anxiety

How much of your attention goes to whether you're about to be left.

Axis II — avoidance

How far you'll go to keep from being fully known.

Eighteen questions. No right answers and no score against anyone else — it plots you as a single point on those two axes. Nearly everything else about how a person loves follows from where that point lands.

Nothing is saved or sent. It runs entirely in this tab and there's no server behind it. Close it and it's gone — unless you choose to copy the shareable link at the end, which carries your answers in its address and nowhere else.

Answer on instinct, not on who you'd like to be. The instrument is only as good as the honesty.

      Now find out what he is.

      Seven chapters. His coordinate, where any of this comes from, why people end up where they end up — and what the two of you specifically are likely to do to each other. Roughly eight minutes.

      Built on the two-factor model behind the Experiences in Close Relationships scale (Brennan, Clark & Shaver; revised by Fraley et al.) — the standard adult attachment instrument. This is a short, informal adaptation: a good mirror and a conversation starter, not a diagnosis. Attachment is a pattern, not a personality, and patterns move.
